### AI-accelerated productivity growth [ai_acceleration]
Generative and later AI systems act as a general-purpose technology whose measured payoff arrives after a J-curve delay, raising world productivity growth from the 2030s.
  assumption: Task-level productivity gains observed in 2023-2025 experiments generalise to a large share of tasks (a strong transfer assumption).
  assumption: Complementary investment and reorganisation delay macro effects until about 2032 (J-curve).
  assumption: Compute and energy supply expand fast enough; energy intensity improves more slowly because of data-centre demand.
  assumption: Gains spread beyond the frontier economies.
